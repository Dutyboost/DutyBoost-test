## Part 2: The Critical Conversion Gap

### The 72% Problem
**Core Finding:** While travelers browse extensively at airports, the majority do not purchase.

#### Why Travelers Don't Convert:
1. **Price Uncertainty** — Unsure if airport prices are competitive vs. downtown/online
2. **Time Pressure** — Limited time before departure gate closes
3. **Payment Friction** — Unfamiliar payment methods, currency concerns
4. **Lack of Incentives** — No compelling reason to buy at airport vs. online
5. **Psychological Friction** — "I'll buy this elsewhere" mentality

#### Market Reality:
- **Travelers spend >33% of airport time on retail activities** (dining, shopping, browsing)
- **But only ~28% convert to purchase** (implied from 72% browse-no-buy statistic)
- **Lost market opportunity:** €18–€25B annually in untapped airport spending

## Part 3: Travel Partner Commission Crisis

### The Declining Revenue Model
Travel partners (OTAs, booking platforms, travel agencies) face relentless commission erosion:

#### Commission Rates by Segment (2024):
| Segment | Commission Rate | Trend |
|---------|-----------------|-------|
| **Airlines** | 0–2% | ↓ Declining |
| **Hotels** | 15–25% (OTA averages) | → Stable but commoditized |
| **Car rentals** | 10–20% | ↓ Decreasing |
| **Travel agencies** | 5–10% | ↓ Eroding |

#### Why Commissions Are Collapsing:
1. **Supplier power shift** — Airlines and hotels prefer direct bookings
2. **OTA saturation** — Expedia, Booking.com, etc. have massive inventory → lower margins
3. **Airfare transparency** — GDS and metasearch engines commoditized flight prices
4. **One-transaction model** — Partners earn only at booking time, not on downstream customer activity

#### Real-World Impact:
- A travel partner issuing **100,000 bookings/year** at **€800 average:**
  - Airline commission (0–2%): **€0–€16,000** annually
  - Hotel/accommodation commission (varies): **€10,000–€40,000** annually
  - **Total passive revenue:** €10,000–€56,000 for massive volume

**The Gap:** Partners need **recurring, scalable revenue** beyond the initial booking commission.
